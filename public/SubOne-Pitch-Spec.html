The gap: There is no tool that lets an international client pay a Vietnamese freelancer directly, instantly, cheaply, and with built-in dispute protection — without either party needing a bank account, a CEX account, or technical crypto knowledge.

2. What SubOne Is

SubOne is a USDC payment wallet built for cross-continental freelance work. It runs on Arc Chain and uses a smart contract escrow to give both client and freelancer trustless protection.

  1. The freelancer creates a wallet, generates a payment request link (name + amount + job description), and shares it.
  2. The client opens the link, connects their existing wallet (MetaMask / WalletConnect / Coinbase), deposits USDC into escrow.
  3. The freelancer completes work, marks it done, USDC is released — confirmed on-chain in under 1 second.
  4. If disputed, an arbiter resolves. If neither party acts, funds auto-release after the agreed window (default 30 days).

No CEX. No bank. No SWIFT. No waiting.

3. Arc Chain Technologies Used

3.1 USDC as Native Gas Token

Arc Chain is unique: USDC is the native gas token. On every other chain (Ethereum, Base, Polygon), users need ETH/MATIC to pay gas — a separate asset the freelancer must acquire before they can do anything.

Impact on SubOne: A freelancer who receives their first USDC payment can immediately send it, approve escrow contracts, or pay out — without ever buying a gas token. Zero onboarding friction.

3.2 Sub-Second Finality

Arc Chain confirms transactions in under 1 second with finality. Compare: Ethereum ~2–3 minutes, Polygon ~2 seconds, Base ~2 seconds.

Impact on SubOne: When a client deposits USDC into escrow, the freelancer sees it confirmed in under a second. The "payment sent" moment feels instant — as fast as a Venmo notification, not a bank wire.

3.3 Predictable, Stable Fees

Because USDC is the gas token, Arc fees are denominated in a stable asset. On Ethereum, gas costs fluctuate with ETH price. On Arc, the fee is a predictable fraction of a cent regardless of market conditions.

Impact on SubOne: The UI shows "Fee: ~$0.50 (0.5%)" and that number is accurate. No surprises at settlement time.

3.4 SubOneEscrow Smart Contract (on Arc Testnet)

Contract address: 0x2fcb5f8c35bd16cb26c151f2ede8020683d5f3f5

  • Job lifecycle: Active → Completed → Released / Disputed / Refunded
  • Per-job fee snapshot: fee locked at job creation (0.5% default, 2% max)
  • Auto-release: if neither party acts within the agreed window, anyone can trigger release to freelancer
  • Dispute window: closes at autoReleaseAt — prevents late disputes on already-released funds
  • Arbiter resolution: designated arbiter (upgradeable to multisig/DAO) resolves disputes
  • No custodian: SubOne itself cannot touch funds — only contract logic moves money

3 rounds of security review before deployment. 5 Critical/High findings fixed.

3.5 Arc RPC + Block Explorer

SubOne reads on-chain state (balances, job list, transaction history) directly from https://rpc.testnet.arc.io using viem. All transaction links point to https://explorer.testnet.arc.io.

4. How SubOne Helps Cross-Continental Freelancers

The Freelancer (Vietnam side)

  1. Opens SubOne, connects MetaMask wallet in 10 seconds.
  2. Taps "Receive" → enters $500 USDC + "UI design, 3 screens" → gets a shareable link.
  3. Sends link to client over Telegram / email / Upwork chat.
  4. Client pays → USDC confirmed in wallet in under 1 second.
  5. Marks job complete → USDC released. Done.

Time to funds: under 1 minute. Fee: 0.5%.

The Client (international side)

  1. Clicks payment link — opens in any browser, no app needed.
  2. Connects existing MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
  3. Approves USDC + creates escrow in 2 transactions (each confirmed in <1s on Arc).
  4. Funds sit in trustless escrow — client can open a dispute if work is not delivered.
  5. Once freelancer marks complete, client releases funds with one tap.

The client does not need to know what Arc Chain is. They just need USDC in a wallet they already own.

Key differentiators in one sentence each

vs. Payoneer / Wise: SubOne settles in seconds, not days, at a fraction of the fee — and does not require a verified bank account.

vs. Upwork: Upwork takes 20% and holds funds. SubOne holds 0.5% and the smart contract holds the rest — neither SubOne nor anyone else can access it.

6. Regulatory Context (Vietnam)

SubOne operates in alignment with Nghị quyết 05/2025/NQ-CP — Vietnam's regulatory sandbox framework for fintech and blockchain applications.

The off-ramp path to VND uses Dragon Lab's MIMO service, which holds sandbox license GP 1724/GP-SKHCN issued by the Da Nang Department of Science & Technology, valid until 17/12/2028. MIMO is non-custodial, uses VNPT AI for eKYC, and requires physical presence at approved locations in Da Nang.

SubOne itself does not hold, exchange, or transmit Vietnamese Dong. It is a USDC payment tool. VND conversion is a separate, complementary service.
